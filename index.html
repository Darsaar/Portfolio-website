The problem

The category head planned to raise Milk and FnV indents by 20% across every Coimbatore store from Monday. Analytics had one weekend to confirm it, using eight weeks of messy store × SKU × day data.

What I did

  • Cleaned the data in DuckDB SQL: 827 duplicate deliveries, 611 cancelled orders, 11 closure days, 5 new stores.
  • Separated the real drop from reporting effects.
  • Split by category and day: only milk fell, overnight on 10 August.
  • Traced it to supplier V03, with Salem as the control group.

The trade-off

Extra milk would have recovered some sales but hidden the supplier problem. FnV was never short, so 91% of the extra FnV would have been thrown away.

Outcome

Modified plan: cancel the +20% indent, escalate to V03, line up a backup milk supplier, track fill rate daily and fix the availability report.

The problem

70% of photos are never revisited, and 79% of searches are one or two words, even when people remember far more. The goal: more people find a half-remembered photo quickly.

What I did

  • Built a 5-stage discovery engine (Python + Gemini) across 7 sources.
  • Turned 53,986 posts into 1,091 real failed-search stories and six hypotheses.
  • Tested them with 46 survey answers and 6 interviews: 3 validated, 2 refined, 1 rejected.
  • Prioritised with impact × ease and MoSCoW.

The trade-off

I put Life Chapters (search by life event) on hold. It needs new AI and raises privacy questions. Memory Match uses parts Google already has and fixes the top two pain points.

Outcome

An MVP with editable tags, the six best photos with a reason for each match, and one follow-up question. North Star: photo opened within 60 seconds, target +15%.

The problem

Build a product 100M Indians use for their daily commute by 2035, without it being a scooter or a motorcycle. 60%+ of households own a two-wheeler and only 7% own a car.

What I did

  • Mapped four commuter personas against every 2035 option.
  • Scored five concepts and chose the hardest, the only one that met every need.

  • Built a price ladder and a city-by-city path to 100M riders.

Saathi carries the most technical risk. Where driverless isn't approved yet, owners drive in manual mode, so the product still works.

Outcome

03Things I built

Two tools I shipped to prove a point

Both are live. The mocks on the right replay what each one does.

Google Photos · search

Memory Match

Describe a photo the way you remember it. Memory Match turns your words into editable tags and shows the best matches, each with the reason it matched.

  • NL → query: a free-text memory is parsed by an LLM into who / where / when / text tags, with a rule-based fallback.
  • Explainable ranking: multi-cue scoring with fuzzy dates; each result shows what matched, was close or missed.
  • Vision check: search words are verified against the photo through a serverless vision-model call before they are saved.

Research · AI pipeline

Discovery engine

A five-stage pipeline that scrapes seven public sources, filters the noise and turns the data into real stories of failed photo searches, so research starts from evidence.

  • Scraping: 7 Python scrapers across Play Store, App Store, Reddit, YouTube API, Help forums, HN and Stack Exchange.
  • LLM extraction: each post is converted into a 12-field JSON schema: cues, query typed, failure stage.
  • Scoring: ranks problems on mentions × severity × source spread × fixability, in a static app with a grounded Ask tab.
