About

Evidence first, then the roadmap.

I studied Chemical Engineering at IIT (BHU), which taught me to break a system into inputs, constraints and outputs. I now apply the same habit to products.

Most recently, I spent a year at Kalliope Consulting on the Nation with Namo campaign. I modelled data on more than a million electors, built a prioritisation model for 1,500 booths and automated reporting with Python scrapers. Before that, I ran pricing and retention experiments as a product analyst intern at Kounselo.

My case studies follow one rule: check the data before trusting the brief. At Blinkit, that meant cancelling a plan to order 20% more stock. At Google Photos, it meant reading 54,000 public posts before writing a single survey question.
